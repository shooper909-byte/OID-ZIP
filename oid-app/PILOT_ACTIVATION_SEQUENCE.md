## Mandatory stop conditions

Stop or do not activate if any of the following occurs:

- An origin, database, Blob account or Key Vault is reachable publicly.
- MFA, user disablement, session revocation or identity-header anti-spoofing fails.
- Any role can exceed `RBAC_MATRIX.md`, Ask OID crosses authorization boundaries, or AI performs a privileged action.
- A failed/unreviewed test can release or allocate a lot, Operations can override Quality, or audit evidence is incomplete.
- An unapproved Critical/High image, dependency or infrastructure finding remains.
- Secrets or sensitive supplier/security/document data appear in logs or artifacts.
- Backup restore, monitoring/alerts, legal/privacy, Quality, security, governance or Shelby approval is missing.
- Actual or forecast spend exceeds the approved ceiling without a new approval.

Stopping is not a failure of the safety system; it is the required control response. Record the stop, preserve evidence, disable access, and return the affected gate to `FAIL` or the applicable non-pass status.

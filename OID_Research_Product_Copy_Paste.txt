SKU STANDARDIZATION — PROPOSED REPLACEMENTS
Pattern: OP-R- plus the website product ID padded to five digits. Example: product ID 12 becomes OP-R-00012. R means research catalog. Each catalog record, kit, bundle, and panel has its own identifier. IDs are identifiers, not strengths. All 83 replacements are unique and use exactly the same pattern; no compound names or abbreviations appear in the new SKUs.
Use the same proposed SKU in WooCommerce and OID when applying the change. Neither system has been changed by this document. Keep the original-to-new mapping for existing orders and integrations. Product identity, ingredients, and strengths remain accurately disclosed. Neutral SKU naming does not establish processor or regulatory approval.
Original export check: all 83 included SKUs were nonblank and unique. Their formats varied (OP-, OPL-, ORP-, kits and stacks). No duplicate SKU correction was needed; this revision standardizes naming.
A source SKU cannot verify the actual chemical, strength, package, or publication status. Missing specifications remain flagged below. In particular, Thymosin Alpha 1 has 10MG in its old SKU but no strength in its exported name.

HOW TO USE
Each entry follows the OID form in left-to-right, top-to-bottom order. Copy the value after each field label. Select dropdown values manually. Bracketed instructions are NOT values to paste.
Product names and original SKUs come from the export; HTML entities in names are decoded. Internal SKU fields below contain PROPOSED replacement SKUs, not the current website SKUs. SKU display names reuse the product name. Canonical names are suggested normalized labels, not verified chemical identities. Product families use an existing export category; research classifications are suggested internal labels.
UNDER_REVIEW is the staging default shown in your screenshot, not a website status or quality approval.
The export does not contain publication status/catalog visibility. These are non-archived research records in your website export, including out-of-stock records; live publication has not been verified.
Physical form and storage requirements are absent for ALL records. Do not accept the form’s default Lyophilized vial without checking the product specification. Leave storage blank if optional until verified. If OID requires a missing field, complete the verified specification before submitting that record.
Package quantity means units in a sales package, NOT stock on hand. Quantity 6 for six-vial kits and 3/6/9 for named bundles comes from their names. Quantity 1 and package unit vial for individual products are proposed conventions requiring confirmation. Other collection counts are unknown.
Strength is the nominal amount per vial, not a dose or kit total. For blends with explicit component amounts, the numeric strength is their combined total; component amounts remain in the name. For water, 10 mL is volume, not a mass strength. Unknown strengths are left unresolved.
NAD+ 500 mg Research Material is included. The separate NAD+ record categorized Longevity/Wellness is excluded as a nutraceutical.
The two Neurobiology Research Panel records have different SKUs (OP-STK-COGNITIVE and ORP-NEURO-001); both are retained. Verify whether they represent distinct configurations before creating duplicate product masters.
This is a manual-entry reference, not an OID import file. No OID records or inventory have been created.
